A little island that lives at the top of your screen.

Kilo Notch turns the top edge of Windows into a notch that plays your music with word-synced lyrics, runs your focus timer, reminds you to drink, keeps files close and checks off your to-dos. Then it blinks, and gets out of the way.

Theme

Everything you glance at, in one place.

Hover the notch and it opens. Move away and it closes. Swipe between pages with the arrows or your scroll wheel.

Music from any player

Spotify, YouTube in your browser, Apple Music and anything else Windows can control. Cover art, seek bar and a live sound wave.

Lyrics, word by word

Syllable-timed lyrics from Better Lyrics, with LRCLIB as a fallback. Lines grow and glow as they're sung; click one to jump there.

Focus timer

Pomodoro with a ring that sweeps live, a soft chime at the end, and the countdown right in the closed notch.

Water reminder

Set your glass and goal; it nudges you during your hours and charts what you drank today.

File shelf

Drag files onto the notch: light rises from below, the notch gulps them in. Drag them back out with a photo preview under your cursor.

To-do with reminders

Jot a task with a time and the notch taps you on the shoulder when it's due.

Your colors

Six gradient presets, a hue slider, gradient width, vibrance and glow, in dark or light. Text flips black or white on its own to stay readable.

Battery & headphones

A quick peek when you plug in, unplug, or your earbuds connect, with their battery when they share it.

A little personality

When nothing is happening the eyes come out. They follow your cursor, doze off when you're away and say hi when you're back.

Light enough to forget it's there.

Native WPF, no browser engine inside. It only draws while something moves, and gives memory back to Windows while it rests.

Questions

What do I need to run it?

Windows 10 (2004 or newer) or Windows 11, and the free .NET 8 Desktop Runtime. Download KiloNotch.exe, run it, and the notch appears at the top of your screen. Its icon sits in the tray for settings and exit.

Which music players work?

Anything that shows up in Windows' own media controls: Spotify, Apple Music, Windows Media Player, and most players in Chrome, Edge and Firefox, including YouTube and YouTube Music.

Why does a song have no lyrics, or only line timing?

Word-timed lyrics come from Better Lyrics' public cache. Songs it hasn't cached fall back to LRCLIB, a free community library that is usually line-synced; the notch then estimates each word's timing. Some newer or local songs aren't in either yet.

Why doesn't the focus page show my city?

It needs Windows location for desktop apps: Settings → Privacy & security → Location → "Let desktop apps access your location". You can switch the city off in Kilo Notch's settings.

Does it slow down games?

No. It steps aside for full-screen apps, games and presentations, and draws nothing while it's hidden or still.

How do I start it with Windows or quit it?

Right-click the notch or its tray icon. "Start with Windows" and "Exit" are right there.

Privacy

Kilo Notch has no accounts and no analytics. Tasks, water log, shelf and settings live in one file in your user folder.

When the lyrics view is open, the song's title, artist and length are sent to Better Lyrics and, if needed, LRCLIB to find lyrics. If "Show my city" is on, your position is rounded to about 1 km before OpenStreetMap's Nominatim turns it into a place name. Nothing else leaves your PC.
