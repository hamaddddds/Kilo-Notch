01 / LISTEN

A song with room to breathe.

Your album and controls stay on the left. Lyrics unfold on the right, with soft motion that follows along. Works with media that Windows can control.

02 / KEEP

A landing spot for loose ends.

Drop files onto the shelf. Leave yourself a task. Pick a reminder in a couple of taps. The little things deserve a good place, too.

03 / PLAY

Three shots. Yours to keep.

A tiny photo booth, complete with a countdown and a printed strip. Kilo, Film or Paper. Switch the frame or filter and print the same shots again.

04 / SAY HELLO

There’s someone in your notch.

Kilo peeks when you type, gets sleepy when power runs low, and perks up when an update arrives. Turn on camera reactions and it can notice when you’re there.

How does it install and update?

Kilo installs for your Windows account, with its own custom setup and uninstall screens. It checks a small JSON feed for updates, verifies the download’s SHA-256 checksum, and opens the custom installer when you choose to update. Tasks, settings and saved photos are kept when you uninstall.

Does everything work offline?

The shelf, tasks, timers and photo booth work locally. Lyrics, update checks and the optional city label need an internet connection. Lyrics availability and timing depend on the song and provider.

What does “camera reactions” mean?

Optional local face presence and position detection. Kilo can look toward you and greet you when you return. It does not identify people or infer emotions. You can switch it off in Settings or the tray; a green indicator shows when it’s on.

Can I see how it’s made?

Yes. Kilo is open source, built with WPF for Windows and C++ for its installer. Explore the repository ↗
